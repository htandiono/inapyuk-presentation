Purwadhika final project

InapYuk

See the price of each night, then book with the owner.

Hendrik and Awan

The idea

The price should be clear before you book.

A lot of booking sites show one number, then change it at the last step. InapYuk puts the price of every night on a calendar. If a holiday weekend costs more, you see that before you pay.

Who it is for

One site, two jobs.

Guest

Search for a stay, book it, upload payment proof, and leave a review after checkout.

Owner

List rooms, set busy dates and peak prices, confirm payment, and read the reports.

In the app, the owner is called a tenant.

How we split the work

Awan opens the door. Hendrik closes the stay.

Awan, Feature 1

Homepage, search, accounts, profiles, and the tools an owner uses to list a place.

Hendrik, Feature 2

Booking, payment proof, reviews, notifications, and the sales and occupancy reports.

Same look, same database. We will demo in that order.

Awan

Finding a place, and putting one online.

I will show the homepage, the catalog, accounts, and the owner’s property tools.

Homepage

Search starts on the first screen.

The guest picks a city, check-in, check-out, and the number of guests.

Under that, a few recommended stays, each with a starting price.

Catalog

Every place, with its own photo and price.

The demo has eight stays in Bali and Yogyakarta.

Search by name, filter by type, and sort by name or price. Paging and filters run on the server.

Property page

Photos, rooms, and a map.

Open a place and you get its photos, a short description, the room choices, and a price calendar.

The pin comes from the address the owner saved.

Rooms and the map

The guest picks a room. The calendar shows the price.

Garden Studio is Rp 750,000 a night. Poolside Suite is Rp 1,450,000.

Hendrik continues from this calendar.

Accounts

Guests and owners sign up separately.

  • A new account gets a verification email. The link works once, and it expires.
  • Forgot password uses the same kind of link.
  • Google sign-in is on the login page. You can also link Google later from your profile.

Profile and listing tools

The owner controls the price the guest sees.

  • Profile: name, photo, password, and email. A new email must be verified first.
  • Listing: category, photos, address, and a map pin you can drag.
  • Each room has free dates and a peak-season price, as a fixed amount or a percent.

Hendrik

From a date on the calendar to a finished stay.

Awan’s calendar already knows the price. I take the guest from that date through payment, review, and the owner’s reports.

Booking

The price is saved night by night.

The guest picks check-in and check-out. Nothing is charged until they send the booking.

We store the price of each night at that moment. If the owner changes the peak price later, this booking stays the same.

Payment

Upload proof, or the booking cancels itself.

  • The guest uploads a photo of the transfer. Jpg or png, up to 1 MB.
  • A countdown runs while we wait for that file.
  • If the file never arrives, the booking is cancelled and the room is free again.

The owner confirms

Accept, reject, or cancel.

Transactions shows only this owner’s bookings. Cancel always asks for confirmation first.

The guest sees a notification in the bell, and gets an email when the payment is accepted. A reminder also goes out the day before check-in.

After checkout

One review per finished stay.

When the checkout date has passed, the stay is marked complete. Then the guest can write one review, and the owner can reply.

Reports

Where the money came from, and which rooms are full.

  • Sales can be grouped by property, by booking, or by guest, and filtered by date.
  • Occupancy is a calendar of which rooms are taken.
  • Both pages work on a phone as well as on a laptop.

What you can check

A few rules we kept the whole way.

  • A guest only sees their own bookings. An owner only sees their own properties.
  • Forms are checked in the browser and again on the server.
  • Lists are filtered, sorted, and paged on the server.

Live demo

Guest books. Owner confirms.

We sign in as a guest, book a room in Bali, and upload proof. Then we sign in as the Bali owner, accept that proof, and open the sales report.
